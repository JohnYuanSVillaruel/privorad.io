Core Capabilities

Automated Attendance & Compliance

Three integrated capabilities engineered for operational accuracy without manual intervention.

Real-Time Attendance Logging

Synchronized dynamic QR tokens regenerate on automated countdown cycles. Students verify attendance through optical viewfinders within milliseconds, appending authenticated records to immutable audit logs.

  • Dynamic rotating session tokens
  • Sub-second camera capture

Early Drop-Risk Alerts

Institutional compliance engines monitor attendance percentages per enrolled course. When attendance drops below 80%, immediate visual alerts notify the student while dispatching early warning notices to faculty.

  • 80% threshold drop detection
  • Persistent student warning banners
  • Automated faculty notifications

Architecture

Three-Step Attendance Workflow

How Privora 2.0 validates physical attendance and protects credit eligibility.

STEP 01
Broadcast Active Session

Teacher initiates session in room console. Privora generates an encrypted, rotating session token broadcasting to the classroom display.

STEP 02
Optical Scan & Verification

Students open their optical scanner. The token is verified against the active session, logging an on-time check-in and token audit hash.

STEP 03
Automated Standing & Alert

Attendance standing is recalculated immediately. If any student falls below the 80% threshold, drop-risk alerts trigger for student and faculty.
